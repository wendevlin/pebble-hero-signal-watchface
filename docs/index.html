Hero Signal!

The watchface

A superhero comic watchface for Pebble. Comic-lettered time, the Vienna skyline, and a hero signal beaming from the Donauturm, which cuts out with a "KRZZT!" when your phone disconnects.

Features

  • Phone connection at a glance: the signal goes out and the watch vibrates when the link drops.
  • Weather drawn into the sky, plus an optional Home Assistant temperature (12°/21°).
  • Steps and battery meters, four hero colours, Pebble Time 2, Round 2, Time and Time Round.
